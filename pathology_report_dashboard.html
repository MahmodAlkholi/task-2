Key takeaways

  • A pathology report is clinical communication, not just a list of findings.
  • Every section has a distinct purpose.
  • Diagnosis must be supported by the evidence.
  • Mandatory elements vary by specimen, disease, country and institution.
Reporting requirements vary

Use the current disease-specific protocol, staging edition, national dataset and local standard operating procedure (SOP).

If you remember one thing

The diagnosis is the conclusion; the other sections establish the evidence, context and clinical consequences.
